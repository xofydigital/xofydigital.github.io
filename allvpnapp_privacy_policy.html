ALLVPN Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the ALLVPN mobile application processes information when you use the application and its VPN services.

Information We Process

The application may process technical information needed to provide and maintain the service, including your IP address, connection details, selected VPN server, session information, and device-related technical data.

Application settings, favorite servers, and other preferences may be stored locally on your device.

How We Use Information

Information is used to operate the VPN service, provide server lists, authenticate users when required, manage subscriptions when enabled, troubleshoot technical problems, and protect the service from abuse.

Service Providers and Third Parties

When you connect to a VPN or API server, that server may receive technical network information, including your IP address and request metadata. Information may also be processed by service providers that help operate authentication, payments, hosting, or infrastructure.

We do not sell your personal information.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Data stored locally is generally removed when you clear the application data or uninstall the application, unless it has been synchronized with another service.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

The application is not intended for children who are not legally permitted to use the service in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal information. To make a request, contact the application operator using the contact details provided with the application or in its store listing.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the processing of your information, contact the ALLVPN application operator through the support contact published with the application.
