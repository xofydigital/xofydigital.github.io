Account and Data Deletion

This page explains how users of the VPNFY application can request deletion of their VPNFY account and associated data.

How to Request Deletion

Deletion request email: vpnfyapp@gmail.com
Send a deletion request
  1. Send an email to vpnfyapp@gmail.com. If possible, send it from the Google Account email address that you used to sign in to VPNFY.
  2. Use the subject line: “VPNFY account deletion”.
  3. In the message, provide the email address associated with your VPNFY account and clearly state that you want your account and associated data to be deleted.
  4. If you send the request from a different address, include the Google Account email used with VPNFY. To prevent unauthorized deletion, we may request additional proof that you own the account.
  5. After verifying the request, we will delete data that we are not legally required to retain and will confirm the outcome by email.

Do not send your Google password, ID tokens, bank card details, CVC/CVV code, or any other payment credentials. They are not required to process a deletion request.

Data That Will Be Deleted

After a request is verified, the following account-related data will be deleted or anonymized unless there is a legal basis for continued retention:

  • Account information Google Account identifier, email address, and display name, if provided.
  • Access and subscription information Paid access status and expiration, last payment date, and subscription status.
  • Free-access usage records Usage dates and the number of seconds used in connection with the account.
  • Associated service data Internal records required to operate the account, unless they must be retained temporarily for payment processing, dispute resolution, security, or legal compliance.

Deleting your VPNFY account does not delete your Google Account or data independently processed by Google, payment providers, banks, or government authorities.

Data That May Be Retained and Retention Periods

Data type Retention period or outcome
Daily free-access usage and pseudonymous device fingerprint Automatically deleted 7 days after the applicable usage date.
VPNFY internal records of successful payment transactions Automatically deleted after 120 days unless longer retention is required to resolve a dispute or comply with the law.
Failed, canceled, or incomplete payment transactions Automatically deleted after 3 days unless a verification is still pending, a dispute exists, or the information must be retained by law.
Payment and fiscal information May be retained to the extent and for the period required by law. Payment providers, banks, and tax authorities apply their own retention periods.
Technical logs and error information Retained until scheduled rotation for the period necessary for security and diagnostics. When investigating an outage, attack, or dispute, retention may be extended for the duration of the investigation.
Backups Deleted data is removed from backups during the ordinary backup refresh cycle.

How to Delete Data Stored on Your Device

An email request applies to data stored on VPNFY servers. Local preferences, favorite servers, split-tunneling settings, technical configurations, and other on-device data can be removed in any of the following ways:

  • sign out in the VPNFY application to remove local Google session data;
  • clear VPNFY application data in Android settings; or
  • uninstall VPNFY from the device.

Consequences of Deletion

After deletion, you may lose the ability to sign in to VPNFY, use any remaining paid access, restore access, view free-access usage history, or receive support for the deleted account. Account deletion does not itself constitute or initiate a refund.
