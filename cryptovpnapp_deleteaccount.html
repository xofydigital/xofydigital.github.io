CryptoVPN Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: September 25, 2026.

This page explains how to request deletion of your CryptoVPN account record and associated data, including after you have uninstalled the app. CryptoVPN does not currently create a username-and-password account. VPN access and trial usage are associated with an app account record.

How to request deletion

  1. Email vpnappcrypto@gmail.com from any email application. You do not need to reinstall CryptoVPN.
  2. Use the subject: CryptoVPN account and data deletion.
  3. Write: “Please delete my CryptoVPN account record and associated data.”
  4. If you know your CryptoVPN device ID, include it. Otherwise, provide approximate dates of use and the country or server you last selected so support can try to locate the correct record. You may submit the request even if you no longer have the device or the app.
  5. Send the message and wait for a reply with any verification steps.

Do not send your device secret, Play Integrity token, VPN configuration, password, full payment card number, or banking code. CryptoVPN does not need these to receive a deletion request.

Verification

Support may ask for the minimum additional information needed to find the record and verify that the request concerns your device. An email address alone may not identify a CryptoVPN record because email is not used as the account identifier. If the available details cannot identify the record reliably, support will explain what additional information is needed rather than deleting another user's data.

Data to be deleted

After the record is located and the request is verified, CryptoVPN will delete or de-identify, where applicable:

  • the app account identifier and related authentication data;
  • trial dates, limits, and traffic usage linked to the account;
  • VPN access settings associated with the account;
  • other service data directly linked to the account.

Sending the email starts a support request; it does not automatically delete data. Simply uninstalling CryptoVPN does not delete account data stored by the service.

Data that may remain

Limited request-handling, security, or infrastructure records may need to be kept when required by law or necessary to protect the service and other users. Support will explain any applicable exception in its reply. A request to CryptoVPN does not remove records independently held by Google, your email provider, DNS providers, or other services under their own policies.

Local data on your device

A remote deletion request cannot erase data stored only on your device, including split-tunneling preferences and the local connection history. To remove it, clear CryptoVPN's app data in Android settings or uninstall the app. You can still request deletion of service-held account data afterward using this page.

Processing time

Once support has enough information to verify the request, deletion is normally completed within 30 calendar days. If a legal retention duty or a verification problem changes that timeframe, support will explain it in the response.

After deletion

You will lose the associated VPN access and trial state. Removed usage history and access settings cannot be restored. If you install CryptoVPN again, the app may create a new account record, subject to the service's then-current access rules.
