How to Request Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your ALLVPN account and associated personal information by email.

Send an Email

Send your request to allvpnplayapp@gmail.com.

What to Include

  1. Use the subject line: Account deletion request.
  2. Provide the email address or account identifier associated with your ALLVPN account.
  3. Send the request from the email address associated with your account whenever possible.

What Happens Next

We may ask for additional information to verify account ownership. After verification, we will process the deletion request and remove the account and associated personal information, except for information that must be retained to comply with legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, or security obligations.

Local Application Data

Data stored only on your device can be removed by clearing the ALLVPN application data or uninstalling the application. This does not replace a request to delete server-side account information.

Contact

If you have questions about account deletion, contact us at allvpnplayapp@gmail.com.
